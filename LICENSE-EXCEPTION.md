## Why this exists

The app is published on the App Store for a small one-time price, which only
covers Apple's annual developer fee. The App Store's terms are widely
considered incompatible with the plain GPL, so this permission makes that
distribution possible without taking anything away from users:

- The full source code stays available under the GPL, version 3.
- Anyone can build the app themselves and sign the `.ipa` for their own
  devices, free of charge.
- The Android builds (GitHub releases, F-Droid, Google Play) are not affected.
