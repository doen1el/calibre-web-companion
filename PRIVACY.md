# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-18_

Calibre Web Companion ("the app") is an open-source client for servers that
you run or have access to yourself, such as Calibre-Web, Calibre-Web Automated
or other OPDS servers. The developer does not operate any server for the app
and does not receive any of your data.

## Data the app collects

None. The app contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash
reporting service.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

- Your server address, user name and password or session cookie, so you stay
  logged in.
- Your settings, downloaded books, cached covers, reading progress and
  bookmarks.
- Optional credentials you enter for other services (for example a WebDAV
  server, a KOReader sync server or metadata API keys).

Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Connections the app makes

The app only connects to services you configure or actively use:

- **Your book server** (Calibre-Web, Calibre-Web Automated, calibre content
  server, OPDS): to browse, download, upload and edit your books.
- **Optional services you set up yourself:** WebDAV (reading progress sync),
  KOReader sync server, BookDrop / Send-to-E-Reader, Shelfmark.
- **ISBN / metadata lookups**, only when you scan or search for a book's
  metadata: depending on your settings Open Library, Google Books, the
  Bibliothèque nationale de France (BnF), Hardcover or ISBNdb. The ISBN or
  search text is sent to the selected service. Their own privacy policies
  apply.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** only to scan a book's ISBN barcode. Images are not stored or
  sent anywhere.
- **Photo library:** only to pick an image as a new book cover, which is then
  uploaded to your own server.
- **Local network:** to reach your server when it runs in your home network.
